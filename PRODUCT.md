# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parents using the school application to select an academic year, sign in, and access information about their children.

## Product Purpose

Provide parents with a clear, dependable path into school services. Success means they can choose the correct academic year and sign in without confusion or unnecessary visual distraction.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, friendly, and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative glass effects, heavy gradients, dark interfaces, dense enterprise styling, and playful visuals that reduce clarity.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action immediately obvious.
2. Use familiar controls and plain language.
3. Keep screens calm, spacious, and focused.
4. Reserve blue for actions, selection, and focus.
5. Maintain visual consistency across authentication screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow general accessibility standards with readable contrast, clear labels, visible focus states, and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.
