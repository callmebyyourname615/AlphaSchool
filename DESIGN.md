# Design System

## Visual Theme

Minimal light product interface for parents. Use a pure white primary surface with restrained royal-blue accents. Avoid background gradients, glow effects, glassmorphism, and decorative shadows.

## Colors

- Primary: `#0756D1`
- Primary light: `#1473E6`
- Primary dark: `#0647B8`
- Text: `#082653`
- Muted text: `#647594`
- Border: `#E3E9F2`
- Soft selected surface: `#F1F6FF`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

Use Inter for Latin text and Noto Sans Lao for Lao text. Headings are bold but compact. Body text remains readable and direct.

## Layout

Use a centered mobile column with a maximum width near 480 pixels. Prefer generous white space and predictable vertical rhythm. Content begins near the top rather than floating in the center.

## Components

- Primary buttons use solid royal blue, 14-16 pixel radius, and no decorative gradient.
- Secondary buttons use white with a subtle neutral border.
- Inputs use white fills, neutral borders, and a blue focus border.
- Selection rows use a subtle blue-tinted surface and blue border only when selected.
- Shadows are minimal and reserved for elevated interactive elements.

## Motion

Use short 150-250 millisecond state transitions only. Avoid staged page-load animation.
